Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At SeatVault, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you register on our platform and buy tickets.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your full name, email address, and authentication credentials when creating an account.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to secure your account, process bookings, display your purchased tickets, and provide customer support.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and booking history for auditing and compliance reasons. You may request account deletion by contacting support, subject to regulatory retention mandates.

4. Payment Security

We minimize our PCI-DSS scope by integrating directly with accredited payment gateways. We never process or store raw credit card details on our servers.
